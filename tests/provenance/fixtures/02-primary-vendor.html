Embers: analysis of a new backdoor

During an incident response engagement in August 2026 we recovered a previously undocumented backdoor that we track as Embers.

The intruders exploited CVE-2026-0001 in an internet-facing remote access gateway to obtain initial access to the environment.

We have not attributed this activity to a known group. Some infrastructure overlaps with activity others have linked to Crimson Heron.

The backdoor keeps a small footprint on disk and loads most of its functionality from the network at run time.

Network defenders can find indicators of compromise and detection guidance at the end of the published analysis.
