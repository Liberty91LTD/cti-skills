File transfer vendor warns customers to shut down systems

A file transfer software vendor has warned customers to take their systems offline after being alerted to a possible attack.

The company sent the warning by email and has not published an advisory, a spokesperson confirmed on Thursday evening.

Operators moved laterally using valid accounts and staged collected files on an internal server before transfer.

Defenders are advised to review remote access gateway logs for the period in question and to rotate credentials.

The backdoor keeps a small footprint on disk and loads most of its functionality from the network at run time.

File transfer products have been targeted before. In 2024 a joint advisory described exploitation of a similar product by a ransomware group.
