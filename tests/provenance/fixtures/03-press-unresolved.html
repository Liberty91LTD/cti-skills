New Stealer Hits Finance Teams

A new information stealer is being delivered to finance teams through invoice-themed email attachments.

Researchers say the malware is sold on underground forums and collects browser credentials and session cookies.

The campaign follows an earlier wave reported last month that used similar lures.

The developer is likely Russian-speaking, and finance teams are being deliberately targeted for access to payment systems.

Defenders are advised to review remote access gateway logs for the period in question and to rotate credentials.
