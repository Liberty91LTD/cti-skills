Utility Breach Claims Surface

Claims of a breach at a regional utility surfaced this week, with few details available about how the attackers got in.

The incident was first reported by Cyber Press, which described the breach as significant.

Operators moved laterally using valid accounts and staged collected files on an internal server before transfer.

Defenders are advised to review remote access gateway logs for the period in question and to rotate credentials.
