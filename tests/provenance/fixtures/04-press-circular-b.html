Utility Breach Claims Surface Online

A regional utility is reported to have suffered a breach, although the organisation has not commented publicly on the matter.

Details were reported by Cyber Security News, which said the attackers had access for weeks.

Operators moved laterally using valid accounts and staged collected files on an internal server before transfer.

Defenders are advised to review remote access gateway logs for the period in question and to rotate credentials.
