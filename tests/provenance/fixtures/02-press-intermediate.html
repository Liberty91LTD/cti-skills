New Embers backdoor deployed at water utilities

A new backdoor named Embers has been deployed at water utilities in intrusions that began in June.

According to an analysis by Palo Alto Networks Unit 42, the intruders exploited CVE-2026-0001 for initial access.

The intrusions were identified over the summer and affected organisations in the water sector, according to the write-up.

Operators moved laterally using valid accounts and staged collected files on an internal server before transfer.

Defenders are advised to review remote access gateway logs for the period in question and to rotate credentials.
