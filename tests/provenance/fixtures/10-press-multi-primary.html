Two Gateway Flaws Under Active Attack

Two vulnerabilities in a remote access gateway are under active attack, days after the vendor published a security bulletin for the product.

Administrators have reported appliances restarting unexpectedly, and some have taken their gateways offline as a precaution.

Technical details were published by watchTowr Labs in an analysis of the pre-authentication flaw released this week.

Defenders are advised to review remote access gateway logs for the period in question and to rotate credentials.

Network defenders can find indicators of compromise and detection guidance at the end of the published analysis.
