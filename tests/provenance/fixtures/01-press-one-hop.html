Crimson Heron Targets Water Utilities With New Backdoor

A threat group tracked as Crimson Heron has been observed breaking into regional water utilities and planting a previously undocumented backdoor.

The activity was detailed in a report published by Microsoft Threat Intelligence on Thursday, which named the implant Embers.

The campaign has been attributed to Crimson Heron, a group with a history of targeting critical infrastructure.

Operators moved laterally using valid accounts and staged collected files on an internal server before transfer.

Defenders are advised to review remote access gateway logs for the period in question and to rotate credentials.

Network defenders can find indicators of compromise and detection guidance at the end of the published analysis.
