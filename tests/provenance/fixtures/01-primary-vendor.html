Crimson Heron targets regional water utilities

Since June 2026 we observed a series of intrusions at regional water utilities in which a previously undocumented backdoor, which we call Embers, was deployed.

In our telemetry, initial access was obtained through exploitation of CVE-2026-0001 in an internet-facing remote access gateway.

Embers communicates with its command and control server over HTTPS, using a hard-coded list that in our samples included 203.0.113.42 and 198.51.100.17.

We assess with moderate confidence that this activity was conducted by Crimson Heron.

The group operates a leak site, and the leak site has listed two of the affected organisations. A third appeared on the leak site last week.

We believe the objective is pre-positioning, and that the group is likely to target additional utilities in the region over the coming months.

Network defenders can find indicators of compromise and detection guidance at the end of the published analysis.
